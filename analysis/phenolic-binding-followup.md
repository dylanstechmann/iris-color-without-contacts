# Closer pigment-binding comparators

The earlier [eleven-reference comparison](binding-benchmark-run-2026-10-17/BENCHMARK_REPORT.md) left the production-up and production-down scaffolds poorly covered. A targeted follow-up found one useful measured phenolic amide comparator, **paracetamol (acetaminophen)**.

## Quantitative input

[Buszman et al., 2009, Table 1, printed page 84](https://czasopisma.umlub.pl/curipms/article/download/2824/2815/7529) reports synthetic DOPA-melanin binding from UV depletion and two-site Scatchard fitting:

| Compound | Site | Association constant, M^-1 | Capacity, nmol/mg melanin | Reciprocal constant, micromolar, calculated here |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Paracetamol | Higher-affinity fit | 450000 | 3.6 | 2.22 |
| Paracetamol | Lower-affinity fit | 24500 | 5.3 | 40.8 |

The reported total fitted capacity is 8.9 nmol/mg. The last column is `1,000,000 / K`, not an independently measured ocular Kd. Commercial drug solution and synthetic pigment were used. Table 1 gives no parameter uncertainties; optical depletion does not establish recovered compound identity or cellular release. These measurements therefore stay separate from the MST and ocular Sips estimates.

Paracetamol supplies a phenolic amide comparator, but does not establish resorcinol or pyrazolone behavior. No affinity is transferred to thiamidol, 4-butylresorcinol or ampyrone.

## Leads that do not enter the quantitative panel

[Patent US20160235642A1, Example 2/Table 1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20160235642A1/en) reports single-condition apparent depletion of catechol and quercetin with synthetic pigment. These are patent disclosure measurements, without affinity or release curves. Chemical transformation, precipitation and optical specificity remain unresolved. Catechol is a positional isomer of resorcinol, which is insufficient to transfer binding behavior.

The targeted search retrieved no qualified direct melanin affinity/capacity/release dataset for thiamidol, 4-butylresorcinol, phenylethylresorcinol, resorcinol, ampyrone, antipyrine, aminopyrine or beta-mangostin. This describes the search outcome, not proof that such data do not exist.

## Next use

Use the paracetamol result to broaden assay and chemical coverage, while retaining endpoint and preparation differences. Seek direct candidate-specific measurements and recovery of unchanged compound. A single-point disappearance measurement cannot supply an off-rate or a tissue-withdrawal timescale.
