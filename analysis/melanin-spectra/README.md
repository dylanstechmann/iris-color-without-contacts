# Melanin extinction-coefficient tables

These are unmodified copies of two melanin extinction-coefficient tables published by the Oregon Medical Laser Center / University of Waterloo Natural Phenomena Simulation Group (Baranoski lab). They were retrieved on November 9, 2026 from the NPSG public data server. Both files are preserved byte-for-byte with LF line endings.

| `sarna_swartz_1988_pheomelanin.csv` | Pheomelanin decadic extinction coefficient, 215–825 nm, 5 nm steps | <https://www.npsg.uwaterloo.ca/data/skin/pheomelanin_extinction.txt> | `7cbd64699fef8e319d1503fd8651f954ba6886ffe6e4d7d8623ab37726488df5` |

Original citation: Sarna T., Swartz H.M., "The physical properties of melanins," in *The Pigmentary System*, ed. J.J. Nordlund et al., Oxford University Press, 1988.

Units: decadic extinction coefficient, cm⁻¹ (mg/ml)⁻¹.

Creator: Natural Phenomena Simulation Group, University of Waterloo. License: research-use public data hosted by the Oregon Medical Laser Center. The project uses these tables for the spectral shape of eumelanin and pheomelanin visible-range absorption; the 550 nm values are normalized to 1.0 inside the consuming models.
