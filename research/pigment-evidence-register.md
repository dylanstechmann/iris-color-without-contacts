# Pigment evidence register

*Reviewed October 11, 2026. Primary studies and official clinical/regulatory sources used in the [tissue atlas](tissue-pigment-atlas.md).*

This is a scoped source register, not a systematic review or a global audit of product availability. Experimental treatment details are omitted. Clinical approvals refer to the stated condition; none of the rows establishes universal cosmetic clearance.

## Skin

| Source | Evidence type / model | Endpoint and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TRI-LUMA prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=a35fa709-5eb5-4429-b38f-f1e0019bf0ee) | Official U.S. prescription label | Short-term moderate-to-severe facial melasma; hydroquinone identified as synthesis inhibitor. Does not establish a general body-color controller. |
| [FDA ruxolitinib vitiligo approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/fda-approves-topical-treatment-addressing-repigmentation-vitiligo-patients-aged-12-and-older) | Official U.S. approval and clinical-trial summary | Disease-specific repigmentation; not arbitrary darkening of healthy skin. |
| [Hakozaki et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12100180/) | Cell/reconstructed epidermis experiments plus human studies | Transfer modulation in models; human facial appearance outcome. Does not prove digestion of existing pigment in people. |
| [Kim et al., 2020; first published 2019](https://doi.org/10.1111/pcmr.12838) | Cultured human melanocytes and melanosome-containing keratinocytes | Melanosome degradation and lighter cell pellets; no clinical iris or body-wide result. |
| [Ebanks et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21326292/) | Cultured human keratinocytes from light- and dark-skin donors | Transferred-melanosome loss differed by donor source; cell model, not a selectable treatment. |
| [Murase et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/jid.2013.165) | Human keratinocytes, cultured ex-vivo human skin, and skin substitutes | Autophagy manipulation changed measured melanin in models; no controlled cosmetic intervention in people. |
| [Correia et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29074272/) | Human melanocore uptake assay in cultured keratinocytes | Melanin localized to hybrid endocytic compartments that were not highly acidic or degradative; model-specific persistence. |
| [Hurbain et al., 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29054596/) | Electron microscopy and tomography of human skin in situ across pigmentation phenotypes | Some keratinocyte melanocore clusters were nonacidic and nondegradative; storage context matters. |
| [Kobayashi et al., 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9579550/) | Human skin explants | Supranuclear melanin caps correlated with fewer UV-induced DNA photoproducts; native protective function is relevant. |
| [Yi et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6056223/) | Physically disrupted human melanocores and cultured keratinocyte model | Intact-core structure supported antioxidant and photoprotective properties; disruption model, not an intervention trial. |
| [Melanophagy study, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10998605/) | Mainly mouse B16F10 melanoma-cell model; transferred-melanosome keratinocyte comparison | Melanophagy machinery; compartment-dependent findings. Does not establish a human treatment. |
| [Marubashi and Fukuda, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10739166/) | Mouse melanocyte/keratinocyte models | Melanosomal protein degradation without altered melanin content; endpoint distinction. |
| [FDA tattoo removal](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/tattoo-removal-options-and-results) | Official U.S. device/consumer information | Cleared laser devices; no approved removal creams. Exogenous dermal pigment is distinct from native melanin. |
| [Maeda, 2017](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9284/4/4/47) | Small human study, six men; inner forearm | Fading of UVA-induced pigmentation used as an epidermal-turnover proxy (36.2 ± 6.2 days); not a direct mass balance for all stored melanin or selective clearance. |
| [Lu et al., 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8881670/) | Human surface corneocytes from exposed and unexposed skin; image analysis | Melanin granules were measured in surface corneocytes and varied with phototype and exposure; documents a superficial pigment pool, not selective removal from the full epidermis. |
| [Matsunaka et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.1111/srt.12309) | Human tape-sampled outer stratum corneum; HPLC; 10 healthy subjects plus a four-person time course | Chemically quantified eumelanin and pheomelanin in surface samples over time; a surface-pool measurement, not whole-tissue mass balance or a clearance intervention. |
| [Choi et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20147966/) | Human in-situ comparison, six subjects with phototypes II–III | Similar visible tanning across exposure conditions did not correspond to the same melanin response; UVA alone produced no significant melanin increase, whereas UVB-containing conditions did. Appearance is not a direct pigment-mass endpoint. |
| [Ito et al., 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7404343/) | HPLC method validation using synthetic melanins, animal/human hair, and human epidermis | Chemically generated marker compounds quantify melanin amount/type in harvested samples; assay-generated products are not evidence of in-vivo catabolites or pigment export. |

## Gums, oral mucosa, and teeth

| Source | Evidence type / model | Endpoint and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [El-Mofty et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33966113/) | Randomized human comparison, 20 participants; topical gel versus injections | Clinical index and histological stain endpoints differ; small study with no placebo arm. |
| [Scalpel/diode-laser trial, 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31983845/) | Human split-mouth scalpel/diode-laser trial, 20 participants | Procedural depigmentation, healing and recurrence; not noninvasive modulation. |
| [Oral pigmented-lesion series](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8141314/) | Retrospective human pathology series | Melanocytic and nonmelanocytic diagnoses; biopsy selection cannot estimate population prevalence. |
| [NIDCR gum disease](https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/health-info/gum-disease) | Official health information | Inflammatory gum signs belong to a separate cause branch. |
| [ADA whitening](https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/whitening) | Official professional clinical information | Dental stains/chromogens and whitening; not evidence for living-tissue melanin clearance. |
| [Thomson et al., 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10577752/) | In-vitro labeling of 105 samples of clinically normal human oral mucosa from several sites | S-phase labeling varied by oral site; measures proliferation, not full epithelial transit or melanin fate. |
| [Thomson et al., 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11684035/) | In-vitro labeling of 110 human mandibular gingiva and buccal mucosa samples | Buccal mucosa had a higher S-phase labeling index than mandibular gingiva; this is not a complete turnover-time or pigment-export measurement. |
| [Squier and Waterhouse, 1967](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-9969(67)90148-3) | Light and electron microscopy of 60 normal human gingival specimens | Melanin granules were observed in gingival keratinocytes; does not quantify melanin in exfoliated cells or establish clearance. |

## Eyes

| Source | Evidence type / model | Endpoint and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Imesch et al., 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8602782/) | Histology of 51 human irides | Stromal melanocyte counts/density did not significantly differ among blue, hazel, and brown groups; cell number is not the main color variable. |
| [Prota et al., 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9778410/) | Human iris microdissection and pigment chemistry | Stromal and posterior epithelial pigment profiles differ; blue irides had very low measured pigment, and cultured cells changed composition with growth/senescence. |
| [UK Xalatan SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/1616/smpc) | Official product information | Susceptibility to drug-associated darkening differs by starting color; cited uniformly blue group had no observed change. |
| [U.S. Xalatan label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f4e73059-5ba0-4d73-9ea1-09d8d654e844) | Official U.S. prescribing information | Increased iris pigment may persist; stopping the drug is not an established reversal method. |
| [FDA eye-drop information](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/buying-using-medicine-safely/what-you-should-know-about-eye-drops) | Official U.S. regulatory information | No approved eye-color-changing drops. |
| [Pfeiffer et al., 2001 (Mainz I)](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaophthalmology/fullarticle/265260) | Human adult-eye and iris immunohistochemistry with PCNA and Ki-67 | No positive nuclei were observed in examined adult iris samples; a histological snapshot does not establish lifetime turnover or pigment export. |
| [Impact of pigment dispersion on trabecular meshwork cells, 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7847180/) | Primary porcine trabecular-meshwork cell model | Pigment exposure reduced phagocytosis and migration and increased cell contraction; not a human cosmetic-clearance study. |
| [IOP elevation precedes phagocytosis decline, 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5915754/) | Porcine ex-vivo pigment-dispersion model | Pigment exposure reduced outflow and raised pressure before phagocytosis declined; effects are model-specific. |
| [Zhou et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34397796/) | Human 15-year follow-up of 21 eyes in 11 patients with pigment-dispersion disease after clinical treatment | Trabecular-meshwork pigmentation attenuated over time; small treated disease cohort, not evidence for cosmetic iris clearance. |
| [Nociari et al., 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3986126/) | RPE cell and mouse models | Selected bisretinoid reduction; does not establish removal of every lipofuscin component. |
| [Lyu et al., 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10194005/) | Mouse RPE and eye-explant research | Lipofuscin degradation mechanism involving melanin; model-specific. |
| [Pfeffer comment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10622903/) and [authors' reply](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10622899/) | Scientific correspondence | Limits and alternative pathways concerning a universal requirement for melanin. No new clinical approval. |
| [Seagle et al., 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1157035/) | Human fetal RPE cultures | Melanin photochemistry/aggregation and light-related apoptosis; function must be considered. |
| [Human choroid mapping study, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6901595/) | Human postmortem tissue and cultures | Distribution and chemical signatures; no cosmetic clearing intervention established. |
| [NLM: pigment epithelium of eye](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/D010857) | Official anatomical vocabulary | Separate iris, ciliary and retinal epithelial compartments. |
| [Postburn conjunctival pigmentation report, 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4251888/) | Human case report | Procedural photoablation; not a general reversible modulator. |

## Systemic and secondary pigmentation

| Source | Evidence type / model | Endpoint and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Multi-tissue minocycline case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9512163/) | Human ophthalmic case report | Skin, sclera, teeth, palate, ears and nailbeds in one patient; mechanism and course after cessation unclear. |
| [Skin, gum, sclera and nail case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36158857/) | Human case report | Multiple visibly affected tissues; case report, not predictable across people. |

## Internal tissues and other pigment classes

| Source | Evidence type / model | Endpoint and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Neuromelanin organelle study, 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29900402/) | Human substantia-nigra tissue characterization | Long-lived autolysosome-like organelles contain undegraded proteins and lipids; no clinical clearance method demonstrated. |
| [Berro et al., 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13408184/) | High-resolution elemental mapping of seven postmortem human locus-coeruleus tissues | Al, Ca, and Fe localized mainly to the pigment compartment; small observational sample, no clearance intervention. |
| [Zecca et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18988735/) | Chemical analysis of human-brain melanic pigments | Neuromelanins contain melanic, lipid, and peptide components and bind metals; composition differs by brain region. |

| [Cardiac lipofuscin study, 2021](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0014482721001178) | Old rats | Reduced cardiac lipofuscin burden; no human clinical inference. |
| [Fibroblast lipofuscin study, 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37438887/) | Stressed human fibroblast cultures | Autophagy activation can accompany increased lipofuscin accumulation under processing failure. |
| [NIDDK hemochromatosis treatment](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/liver-disease/hemochromatosis/treatment) | Official clinical information | Excess iron depletion/chelation for disease; prior damage can persist. |
| [Ranganath et al., pigment regression, 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32904992/) | Human observational alkaptonuria cohort and ear-cartilage biopsies | Partial measured regression; not full reversal or evidence about normal iris melanin. |
| [SONIA 2, 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32822600/) | Randomized human alkaptonuria trial | Precursor lowering and slower disease progression. |
| [FDA Harliku approval](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/opdlisting/oopd/detailedIndex.cfm?cfgridkey=942723) | Official U.S. approval record, June 10, 2025 | Reduction of urinary homogentisic acid in adult alkaptonuria. |
| [NIH Recognizing Jaundice](https://newsinhealth.nih.gov/2022/10/recognizing-jaundice) | Official health information | Bilirubin-related color and cause-dependent medical management. |
| [NHLBI hemoglobin and red cells](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/anemia/causes) | Official physiological information | Blood-cell functions and hemoglobin; vascular color is a separate category from stored melanin. |

## Unassessed compartments

Dedicated studies of clearance in skeletal muscle, adipose, bone, tendon, and every other mucosal surface have not been assembled here. A source in one tissue does not fill these gaps. Future entries should state the chemical substrate, cell type, model, clinical indication, actual pigment endpoint, functional endpoint, and reversibility evidence.
