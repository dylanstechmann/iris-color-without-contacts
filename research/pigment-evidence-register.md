# Pigment evidence register

*Reviewed October 11, 2026. Primary studies and official clinical/regulatory sources used in the [tissue atlas](tissue-pigment-atlas.md).*

This is a scoped source register, not a systematic review or a global audit of product availability. Experimental treatment details are omitted. Clinical approvals refer to the stated condition; none of the rows establishes universal cosmetic clearance.

## Skin

| Source | Evidence type / model | Endpoint and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TRI-LUMA prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=a35fa709-5eb5-4429-b38f-f1e0019bf0ee) | Official U.S. prescription label | Short-term moderate-to-severe facial melasma; hydroquinone identified as synthesis inhibitor. Does not establish a general body-color controller. |
| [FDA ruxolitinib vitiligo approval](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/fda-approves-topical-treatment-addressing-repigmentation-vitiligo-patients-aged-12-and-older) | Official U.S. approval and clinical-trial summary | Disease-specific repigmentation; not arbitrary darkening of healthy skin. |
| [Hakozaki et al., 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12100180/) | Cell/reconstructed epidermis experiments plus human studies | Transfer modulation in models; human facial appearance outcome. Does not prove digestion of existing pigment in people. |
| [Kim et al., 2020; first published 2019](https://doi.org/10.1111/pcmr.12838) | Cultured human melanocytes and melanosome-containing keratinocytes | Melanosome degradation and lighter cell pellets; no clinical iris or body-wide result. |
| [Melanophagy study, 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10998605/) | Mainly mouse B16F10 melanoma-cell model; transferred-melanosome keratinocyte comparison | Melanophagy machinery; compartment-dependent findings. Does not establish a human treatment. |
| [Marubashi and Fukuda, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10739166/) | Mouse melanocyte/keratinocyte models | Melanosomal protein degradation without altered melanin content; endpoint distinction. |
| [FDA tattoo removal](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/tattoo-removal-options-and-results) | Official U.S. device/consumer information | Cleared laser devices; no approved removal creams. Exogenous dermal pigment is distinct from native melanin. |

## Gums, oral mucosa, and teeth

| Source | Evidence type / model | Endpoint and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [El-Mofty et al., 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33966113/) | Randomized human comparison, 20 participants; topical gel versus injections | Clinical index and histological stain endpoints differ; small study with no placebo arm. |
| [Scalpel/diode-laser trial, 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31983845/) | Human split-mouth scalpel/diode-laser trial, 20 participants | Procedural depigmentation, healing and recurrence; not noninvasive modulation. |
| [Oral pigmented-lesion series](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8141314/) | Retrospective human pathology series | Melanocytic and nonmelanocytic diagnoses; biopsy selection cannot estimate population prevalence. |
| [NIDCR gum disease](https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/health-info/gum-disease) | Official health information | Inflammatory gum signs belong to a separate cause branch. |
| [ADA whitening](https://www.ada.org/resources/ada-library/oral-health-topics/whitening) | Official professional clinical information | Dental stains/chromogens and whitening; not evidence for living-tissue melanin clearance. |

## Eyes

| Source | Evidence type / model | Endpoint and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Imesch et al., 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8602783/) | Human iris electron microscopy | Stromal melanocyte/melanosome relation to color; anatomical evidence. |
| [Prota et al., 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9778410/) | Human iris microdissection and pigment chemistry | Stromal and posterior epithelial pigment fractions differ. |
| [UK Xalatan SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/1616/smpc) | Official product information | Susceptibility to drug-associated darkening differs by starting color; cited uniformly blue group had no observed change. |
| [U.S. Xalatan label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f4e73059-5ba0-4d73-9ea1-09d8d654e844) | Official U.S. prescribing information | Increased iris pigment may persist; stopping the drug is not an established reversal method. |
| [FDA eye-drop information](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/buying-using-medicine-safely/what-you-should-know-about-eye-drops) | Official U.S. regulatory information | No approved eye-color-changing drops. |
| [Nociari et al., 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3986126/) | RPE cell and mouse models | Selected bisretinoid reduction; does not establish removal of every lipofuscin component. |
| [Lyu et al., 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10194005/) | Mouse RPE and eye-explant research | Lipofuscin degradation mechanism involving melanin; model-specific. |
| [Pfeffer comment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10622903/) and [authors' reply](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10622899/) | Scientific correspondence | Limits and alternative pathways concerning a universal requirement for melanin. No new clinical approval. |
| [Seagle et al., 2005](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1157035/) | Human fetal RPE cultures | Melanin photochemistry/aggregation and light-related apoptosis; function must be considered. |
| [Human choroid mapping study, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6901595/) | Human postmortem tissue and cultures | Distribution and chemical signatures; no cosmetic clearing intervention established. |
| [NLM: pigment epithelium of eye](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/D010857) | Official anatomical vocabulary | Separate iris, ciliary and retinal epithelial compartments. |
| [Postburn conjunctival pigmentation report, 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4251888/) | Human case report | Procedural photoablation; not a general reversible modulator. |

## Unassessed compartments

Dedicated studies of clearance in skeletal muscle, adipose, bone, tendon, and every other mucosal surface have not been assembled here. A source in one tissue does not fill these gaps. Future entries should state the chemical substrate, cell type, model, clinical indication, actual pigment endpoint, functional endpoint, and reversibility evidence.
