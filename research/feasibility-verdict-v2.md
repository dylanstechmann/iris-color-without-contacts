## Purpose and traceability rule

This document re-ranks the five mechanism-shortlist branches on new data collected through the G1–G7 program. Every re-ranking change from the v1 baseline must cite a dataset in `analysis/` or a registered row in `research/pigment-evidence-register.md`. Vibes-based re-ranking is not permitted. For v1-era framing of the bidirectional goal, see `research/bidirectional-reversibility.md`.

### v1-to-v2 re-rank rulebook

**Exact traceability rule:** Every re-ranking change from the v1 baseline must cite either (i) a dataset in `analysis/` with a committed `receipt.json` pinning input SHA-256s, or (ii) a registered row in `research/pigment-evidence-register.md` that meets the register's stated source bar (primary study or official clinical/regulatory source; non-peer-reviewed sources and conference abstracts are flagged, not barred, but must carry the flag). Citing an unregistered secondary source, a commercial product page, or a retracted paper is not permitted.

**Evidence-class ranking:** `measured` > `registered` > `none`.
- `measured`: a dataset generated by this project's analysis pipeline (washout fits, optics calibration, isotope-ratio assay, etc.) with a committed `receipt.json`. Highest class; overrides registered evidence if they conflict.
- `registered`: a cited row in `research/pigment-evidence-register.md` that has been verified against the primary source or official label. Intermediate class.
- `none`: no cited source. Cannot support a re-rank. Lowest class.

**Unchanged rank entry:** If a branch retains its v1 rank, the **Re-rank** column must state `Unchanged` and the **Rationale** column must record why no re-rank occurred — for example, "No new measured data since v1; registered evidence unchanged" or "New registered evidence exists but is lower class than existing measured data and does not alter rank."

## Predeclared go/no-go criteria for a Phase-2 (pre-ocular) program

The owner must fill the blanks before the M6 review. These thresholds define whether the program advances.

- [ ] **Production-down recovery.** PROPOSED (owner to confirm): Comparative washout half-times (with CIs) for KT-939 vs thiamidol in one human pigment-cell system differ by less than **50 %** — **G1** supplies: *Rationale:* The registered KT-939 1 μM recovers by day 7 and the registered Mann 2018 thiamidol recovers within one week, bracketing a similar effective washout window; a 50 % tolerance accommodates inter-donor assay variation while still flagging scaffold-dependent retention (which would predict a substantially larger separation, with the registered SIK ~2-week reversal as the "functionally different" outer bound) — [`research/pigment-evidence-register.md` rows: Hou et al., 2025; Mann et al., 2018; Mujahid et al., 2017; `analysis/withdrawal-and-recovery.md` §3].
- [ ] **Production-down scaffold retention.** PROPOSED (owner to confirm): Free-compound-corrected synthesis-flux recovers to ≥ **80 %** of baseline by day **14** after withdrawal — **G1** supplies: *Rationale:* The registered KT-939 day-3-to-11 series shows total-pigment recovery approaching baseline by day 11, and the registered Mann 2018 thiamidol recovers within one week; because synthesis flux is expected to recover at least as fast as total pigment for direct reversible inhibitors, an 80 % threshold by day 14 is conservative relative to these registered windows and sets a quantitative synthesis floor for the scaffold-retention test — [`research/pigment-evidence-register.md` rows: Hou et al., 2025; Mann et al., 2018; `analysis/withdrawal-and-recovery.md` §3].
- [ ] **Production-up recovery.** PROPOSED (owner to confirm): At least one production-up candidate returns to within **20 %** of baseline pigment content within **21** days of withdrawal — **G4** supplies: *Rationale:* The registered SIK inhibitor (Mujahid et al., 2017) is the only production-up candidate with a measured reversal precedent (~two weeks in mouse skin); 21 days brackets that window with sampling-grid margin, and the 20 % tolerance is consistent with the registered ampyrone 3D epidermis darkening magnitude (roughly half the positive control) while allowing for partial decay if product handling differs — [`research/pigment-evidence-register.md` rows: Mujahid et al., 2017; Dolinska et al., 2026].
- [ ] **Old-pigment detection.** PROPOSED (owner to confirm): Heavy/light melanin isotope-ratio separation detection limit is ≤ **10 %** of total melanin signal — **G2** supplies: *Rationale:* The identifiability counterexample in `analysis/withdrawal-and-recovery.md` uses a fixed persistent pool of 20 % of initial inventory (p=0.2); a detection limit ≤10 % is required to empirically distinguish that case from a fully replaceable pool, and the 7-day heavy-to-light chase schedule in `research/core-facility-quote-request.md` Gate 1 aligns with the model's native-loss rate scale — [`analysis/withdrawal-and-recovery.md` §4; `research/core-facility-quote-request.md` §5 Gate 1].
- [ ] **Optics tolerance.** PROPOSED (owner to confirm): Inventory→appearance model reproduces published calibration spectra within ΔE*ab ≤ **5.0** — **G3** supplies: *Rationale:* The Phase 3 optics v1 specification already records ΔE ≤ 5.0 as the conventional just-noticeable-difference bound in `analysis/optics-v1-measured-melanin-run-2026-11-09/assumptions.json` and `MODEL_REPORT.md`; the model currently cannot evaluate it because published calibration spectra are paywalled, but the threshold itself is pre-declared in-repo and awaits data — [`analysis/optics-v1-measured-melanin-run-2026-11-09/assumptions.json`; `analysis/optics-v1-measured-melanin-run-2026-11-09/MODEL_REPORT.md` §4].
- [ ] **Iris-cell bridge.** PROPOSED (owner to confirm): Native iris-melanocyte study registered, or sourced-line QC pass rate ≥ **67 %** (≥2 of 3 donor cultures maintain MLANA+/TYR+ marker confirmation and ≥70 % viability through passage 3) — **G5** supplies: *Rationale:* The core-facility quote request and paired protocol already require ≥3 independent donors for primary melanocytes; a ≥67 % pass rate is the minimum consistent with that replication standard. No iris-specific QC dataset exists in the register, so this threshold is planning-grade and should be tightened or replaced by a registered native iris-melanocyte study if one is found — [`research/core-facility-quote-request.md` §6; `research/paired-exposure-withdrawal-protocol.md` §2].
- [ ] **Traceability gate.** PROPOSED (owner to confirm): Every re-ranking row above cites ≥ 1 `analysis/` dataset or ≥ 1 `research/pigment-evidence-register.md` row — **G7** supplies: **pass** (documentary criterion; requires 100 % row compliance, not a numerical threshold).
- [ ] **Cell viability.** PROPOSED (owner to confirm): Viability at efficacious exposure remains ≥ **70 %** of vehicle control in ≥ **2 of 3** independent lines/donors — **G1/G4** supplies: *Rationale:* The core-facility quote-request Gate 3 already sets a ≥70 % viability threshold at the highest soluble concentration, and the registered Dolinska et al., 2026 ampyrone precedent reports no detected apoptosis at the tested concentration; requiring this in ≥2 of 3 donors matches the "≥3 independent donors" replication standard declared in the quote request and protocol — [`research/core-facility-quote-request.md` §5 Gate 3; `research/pigment-evidence-register.md` row: Dolinska et al., 2026].
